A quote is a set of prices agreed with a [company](/guides/companies/) for a stretch of time. It has
a name, lines (a SKU, **the most the buyer may order at that price**, and the negotiated unit price),
the member it is for, notes and an expiry day. Prices are in the base currency, `USD`, like
[price books](/guides/price-books/) and budgets; an order in another currency converts them at its
rate.

A quote is not a [cart's](/guides/carts/) pricing snapshot. A cart's quote is what a cart would
cost now; a negotiated quote is a record of its own that outlives any order.

## Lifecycle

A quote moves only along the moves listed under [Quote](/reference/lifecycles/#quote) in the status
lifecycles: a draft is sent or withdrawn (`declined`), a sent quote is accepted, declined, ended
(`expired`) or superseded by a revision, and an accepted quote can only end early.

**Only a draft can be edited.** A sent quote is read-only; to change it, **revise** it. A revision
starts the next version as a new draft with the same lines, and the version it came from becomes
`superseded`, which is read-only for good. The quote page names every version of the negotiation,
and the list shows each negotiation once, at its latest version. An accepted quote is a deal: it is
not revised, only ended (`expire_quote`) so a new one can be made.

A sent or accepted quote past its expiry reads as **expired** without anything having to run.
Accepting a quote that has lapsed refuses and records the lapse. Each move is made only from the
status it was read in, so an accept and a decline sent at the same moment have one winner, and two
revisions of one quote make one new version.

Staff record the company's answer: **Record acceptance** and **Decline** on the quote's page, or
`accept_quote` and `decline_quote`. Both are audited with who recorded them.

## Drafting and sending

A draft needs at least one line, **one line per SKU** (a quote prices a SKU once, so a smaller order
can't pick up a deeper tier's price), SKUs that are products, and an active company. The buyer, when
named, must be an active member of the company. These are checked when the draft is saved and again
when it is sent. Each line keeps the list price it was written against, so the quote can show what
it takes off.

Sending needs an **expiry day**: the last day (UTC) the prices count, through the end of that day.
The day has to be ahead. A quote has no price until it is accepted.

Operators draft and edit quotes on the **Quotes** page (`/quotes`), where each draft opens in an
editor that works on a phone. The list is newest first and narrows by status, company and a part of
the name. Drafting, editing and revising ask for no confirmation, since a draft prices nothing;
sending, accepting, declining, ending and converting do.

## Pricing from an accepted quote

While a quote is **accepted and before it expires**, its lines are the company's prices. The
[pricing chain](/reference/expressions/#pricing-chain) has a `quote` source ahead of every other
(price books, contract prices, volume tiers) and it stops the chain, so a negotiated price is the
price even when another source is cheaper. A line prices from a quote when:

- the order is from the quote's company (an agentic checkout buyer has no customer, so no quote),
- the line's SKU is on the quote and its quantity is **at most** the quantity on the quote line, and
- the node's **Contract pricing** option is on (as for contract prices).

An order for more than the quoted quantity is priced as if there were no quote, and a quote that
isn't accepted, has expired or was ended prices nothing. Where several accepted quotes price the same
SKU, the lowest price wins. The quote applies to **every** order of the company while it is in force,
not only the order made from it, and the quantity on a line is a ceiling per order line, not a total
the company draws down. The price node's output names the quote that priced a line.

An order already priced keeps its price; ending a quote doesn't reprice it.

## Converting a quote

An accepted quote that hasn't expired is converted once, to one of two things:

- **A purchase order draft**, when the company uses [purchase orders](/guides/purchase-orders/) (it
  has any). The draft names the quote, carries its lines with the quoted prices as the buyer's
  expected ones, and takes a PO number you give (`poNumber`) or one made from the quote. It starts
  nothing: submitting it runs the PO flow, whose price node reads the quote, so the order is priced
  at the quote and still goes through credit, budgets and approvals.
- **An order**, for any other company, on the B2B order flow (or the flow you name). The order's lines
  carry no price, so the quote is what prices it.

`to` overrides the choice. Converting again returns what the first conversion made and makes
nothing new, however often it is asked and however many callers ask at once. The quote stays
accepted, so its price keeps applying until it expires.

## API and assistant

| Tool | Role | Confirms | REST |
|---|---|---|---|
| `list_quotes` | viewer | no | `GET /api/v1/quotes` |
| `get_quote` | viewer | no | `GET /api/v1/quotes/:quoteId` |
| `create_quote` | operator | no | `POST /api/v1/quotes` |
| `update_quote` | operator | no | `POST /api/v1/quotes/:quoteId/update` |
| `revise_quote` | operator | no | `POST /api/v1/quotes/:quoteId/revise` |
| `send_quote` | operator | yes | `POST /api/v1/quotes/:quoteId/send` |
| `accept_quote` | operator | yes | `POST /api/v1/quotes/:quoteId/accept` |
| `decline_quote` | operator | yes | `POST /api/v1/quotes/:quoteId/decline` |
| `expire_quote` | operator | yes | `POST /api/v1/quotes/:quoteId/expire` |
| `convert_quote` | operator | yes | `POST /api/v1/quotes/:quoteId/convert` |

The list takes `companyId`, `status`, `rootId` (every version of one negotiation, superseded ones
included), `search` (part of the name), `cursor` and `limit` (25 by default, at most 100), and
answers `quotes`, `total` and the next `cursor`. A quote read by id carries its `lines` and its
`versions`. The two transports run the same code; see the [MCP](/reference/mcp/#quotes) and
[REST](/reference/rest-api/) references. Each move writes an audit entry (`quote.created`,
`quote.updated`, `quote.sent`, `quote.accepted`, `quote.declined`, `quote.expired`, `quote.revised`,
`quote.converted`).
