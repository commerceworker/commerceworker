A business customer is a company. It has a status, a legal name and tax id, and optionally a parent
company, and it has members and teams. Consumers and internal accounts have no company. Everything
else about a business customer (its balance, credit limit, terms, contract prices, tax
certificates) stays on the customer.

Operators work with companies on a customer's page (linked from **Customers** on **Commerce**), over
the [REST API](/reference/rest-api/) and through the [assistant](/guides/assistant/) and
[MCP](/reference/mcp/#companies). Every change asks first on a confirm card.

## Adding and changing a customer

**New customer** on **Commerce** opens a form: name, email, kind, pricing tier, payment terms and,
for a business customer, an optional legal name and tax id. A business customer is added with its
company, **active** at once. It starts with no credit limit, and the credit limit isn't edited
here. Two customers can't share an email.

**Edit** on the customer's page changes the name, email, kind, tier and terms. A new tier or kind
applies to orders from then on; orders already priced keep their price. A consumer made a business
customer gets a company; a business customer can change kind only once its company is closed or
rejected (and a closed or rejected company can't become a business customer's again).

## Status

A company moves along this lifecycle, and only along it:

| From | To |
|---|---|
| Pending | Active, Rejected |
| Active | Suspended, Closed |
| Suspended | Active, Closed |
| Rejected, Closed | Final |

**Approve**, **Reject**, **Suspend**, **Reinstate** and **Close** on the company's card are those
moves. If two people move the same company at once, one move lands and the other is told the
company changed. An order from a company that isn't **active** is declined at its Validate step
("Company … isn't active (suspended)"); orders already running carry on.

## Members and roles

A member is a person who buys, approves or administers for a company. Members are added by email,
unique within a company. Each has a role, an optional team, an optional **order limit** (the most
one order of theirs may total, in the base currency), and a status:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Invited | Added, not yet active. The start for a person who hasn't been confirmed |
| Active | Can be the buyer on an order |
| Inactive | Paused; can be resumed |
| Removed | Final. The record stays for the orders they placed, and the same email can be added again as a new member |

Roles are fixed: **Buyer**, **Approver** and **Company admin**. Members don't sign in yet and
have no tokens of their own: staff manage them, and an order names the member placing it.

An order names its buyer with `meta.buyerId`, the member's id. The Validate step declines an order
whose buyer isn't an **active** member of the order's own customer. An order with no
`meta.buyerId` is accepted as before. The order limit and the role aren't enforced by Validate: they
are data for the flow's own conditions (below).

Members are personal data. A [data subject export and erasure](/reference/rest-api/#data-subject-requests)
cover them, the retention setting that clears old buyers from orders also blanks the name and email
of members removed that long ago, and the audit trail counts members and names no one.

## Teams

A company's teams form a tree up to six deep. Add one with **Add team**; **Edit** renames a team or
moves it (and the teams under it) beneath another. A member belongs to at most one team.

## What flows and promotions read

A flow's expressions, and a [promotion's condition](/guides/promotions/#conditions), read the company and the
buyer with the names below. Each is `null` for an order with no company or no buyer.

| Variable | Holds |
|---|---|
| `company.status` | `pending`, `active`, `suspended`, `rejected` or `closed` |
| `company.tier` | The customer's pricing tier |
| `buyer.role` | `buyer`, `approver` or `company_admin` |
| `buyer.team` | The id of the buyer's team |
| `buyer.orderLimit` | The buyer's own order limit in the base currency |

For example, a Condition step that sends an order above its buyer's limit to an approval:

```json
{ "and": [
  { "!=": [{ "var": "buyer.orderLimit" }, null] },
  { ">": [{ "var": "order.baseTotal" }, { "var": "buyer.orderLimit" }] }
] }
```

A flow reads these as they stood when the order started its run.
