A purchase order (PO) is a company buyer's own order, kept as a record that outlives the order it
starts. It belongs to a [company](/guides/companies/) and, when it names one, the member who placed
it. It has a PO number (the buyer's own reference), lines, a ship-to address, notes and a status.

Three kinds of PO are records: one drafted in the app or over the API, an [EDI 850](/guides/channel-adapters/)
from a trading partner, and a PO that arrives by [email](/guides/email-intake/). The **Generate PO**
step in a flow is a different thing: it files a document to a supplier and is not recorded here. POs
that come in through [punchout](/guides/punchout/) are not recorded yet.

## Lifecycle

| From | Can become |
|---|---|
| `draft` | `submitted`, `cancelled` |
| `submitted` | `pending_approval`, `approved`, `rejected`, `cancelled` |
| `pending_approval` | `approved`, `rejected`, `cancelled` |
| `approved` | `ordered` |
| `ordered` | `fulfilled`, `cancelled` (when its order is cancelled) |
| `rejected`, `fulfilled`, `cancelled` | nothing: they are final |

**Only a draft can be edited.** Once a PO is submitted it has started an order, so a change is
refused; cancel it and draft another. Each move is made only from the status it was read in, so a
submit and a cancel sent at the same moment have one winner.

A PO number is unique within its company, whatever the letter case. A cancelled PO gives its number
back, so a corrected PO can use it again. Another company may use the same number.

## Drafting

A draft needs at least one line (a SKU and a quantity, optionally a name and the unit price the buyer
expects: the flow prices the order, not the PO), a number, and an active company. A buyer, when
named, must be an active member of that company. These are checked when the draft is saved and again
when it is submitted, since the company or buyer may have changed in between. A PO has no total: the
order it starts is priced, and its page shows the amount.

Operators draft and edit POs on the **Purchase orders** page (`/purchase-orders`), where each draft
opens in an editor, and from a company's customer page, which has a **Purchase orders** section. The
list is newest first and narrows by company, status, buyer, a part of the PO number, and the dates
created. Drafting and editing a draft ask for no confirmation, since a draft starts nothing.

## Submitting

Submitting moves the draft to `submitted` and starts an order on the **B2B purchase order** built-in
flow, carrying the PO number, the buyer and the lines. It starts one order per PO however many times
it is submitted: submitting again returns the same order, and finishes the start if it didn't
complete the first time. The flow:

1. Validates the company and buyer, prices from the company's [price books](/guides/price-books/) and
   calculates tax.
2. Takes the order's total as a credit hold, then checks it against the company's and team's
   [budgets](/guides/companies/#budgets) and records the spend.
3. Asks a company approver to approve when the order is over its credit limit or a strict budget, or
   when its total is over $5,000 in the base currency (a number in the flow's condition, which you
   can change in your copy). Company approvers [vote](/guides/approvals-and-events/); the approval
   escalates to a decline after three days with no decision.
4. Reserves stock, invoices on net 30 terms with the PO number on the invoice, and ships.

See [Built-in flows](/guides/templates/) for the whole graph.

## The PO follows the order

From submission on, the order's progress is the PO's status:

| The order | The PO |
|---|---|
| waits for an approval | `pending_approval` |
| has no approval step left to wait for and is going ahead | `approved`, then `ordered` (a PO that needed no approval passes through `approved`) |
| completes | `fulfilled` |
| is declined while the PO waits for approval | `rejected` |
| is declined after that, is cancelled, fails or rolls back | `cancelled` |

Nothing but cancelling moves a PO past `submitted` by hand. **Cancelling** a PO cancels its order if
it is still running, which gives back the credit hold, the budget spend and the reserved stock. A PO
whose order has already ended can't be cancelled; its status says how it ended.

## POs from EDI and email

A PO in an [EDI 850](/guides/channel-adapters/) from a configured trading partner is recorded as
`submitted` with source `edi`, with the lines and ship-to from the document, and follows its order
like any other. A PO emailed from a known company's address is recorded as `submitted` with source
`email`. The extract step reads the PO number from the document after the record is made, so an
emailed PO's number starts as `EML-` and ten characters taken from the message; the number read from
the document is on the order's variables, and the record keeps the provisional one for now. An email
PO has no lines on its record until a later change reads them in. A PO that can't be recorded (a
customer with no company, or a number the company already has) does not stop its order: the order
runs without a record.

## API and assistant

| Tool | Role | Confirms | REST |
|---|---|---|---|
| `list_purchase_orders` | viewer | no | `GET /api/v1/purchase-orders` |
| `get_purchase_order` | viewer | no | `GET /api/v1/purchase-orders/:poId` |
| `create_po_draft` | operator | no | `POST /api/v1/purchase-orders` |
| `update_po_draft` | operator | no | `POST /api/v1/purchase-orders/:poId/update` |
| `submit_po` | operator | yes | `POST /api/v1/purchase-orders/:poId/submit` |
| `cancel_po` | operator | yes | `POST /api/v1/purchase-orders/:poId/cancel` |

The list takes `companyId`, `status`, `buyerId`, `search` (part of the PO number), `from` and `to`
(days, `YYYY-MM-DD`, UTC, both inclusive), `cursor` and `limit` (25 by default, at most 100), and
answers `purchaseOrders`, `total` and the next `cursor`. Over MCP and REST the two transports run the
same code; see the [MCP](/reference/mcp/#purchase-orders) and [REST](/reference/rest-api/) references.
Drafting, editing, submitting and cancelling write audit entries (`purchase_order.created`,
`purchase_order.updated`, `purchase_order.submitted`, `purchase_order.cancelled`); a PO recorded from
EDI or email writes `purchase_order.received`.
