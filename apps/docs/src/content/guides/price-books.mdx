A price book is a named list of prices: a SKU at a price, or a percentage off list, with volume
tiers for larger quantities. A book is assigned to one customer, or to every customer of a tier,
and has a priority and optional dates. A flow's [Price lines](/reference/nodes/#commerce.price) step
reads the books assigned to the order's customer in the same lookup as the rest of the
[pricing chain](/reference/expressions/#pricing-chain), so carts, orders and agentic checkout price
from them the same way. Prices in a book are in the base currency, `USD`; an order in another
currency converts them at its rate.

## Which book prices a line

A book prices a line only when it is **in force**: active, and inside its dates (from its first
moment up to, not including, its end). Of the books assigned to the customer, by id or through their
tier, that are in force and have an entry for the line's SKU and quantity:

1. The **highest priority** wins, however cheap another book is.
2. Within it, the **lowest price** wins, so the deepest volume tier the quantity reaches is the one used.
3. Books of equal priority and price are told apart by their code, so the same order always prices
   the same way.

A book with no entry for a SKU doesn't shadow a lower-priority book that has one, and a volume tier
that the quantity doesn't reach doesn't count. The rest of the chain still applies: an accepted
[quote](/guides/quotes/) of the customer's company comes first and stops it, then the price-book
source stops processing by default, then contract prices, then volume tiers, then the list price. Price
rules that belong to no book (older entries) still work and rank below every book. A priced line
names the book (its **Price lines** output lists the line's source, the book's name as the rule, and
the book's id).

An agentic checkout buyer isn't one of your customers, so no assigned book applies to it: it pays
list price, or the entries that name no customer.

## Lifecycle

| Status | Means |
|---|---|
| **Draft** | Being built. Prices nothing |
| **Active** | In force while inside its dates |
| **Inactive** | Switched off; can be switched back on |
| **Archived** | Retired for good. Can't change or be assigned |

A book starts as a draft and moves along the [price book lifecycle](/reference/lifecycles/#price-book);
a draft that was never used can be archived directly. Two people changing the same book at once can't both succeed: the
second is told it changed and to read it again.

## Managing books

Operators manage books on **Price books** (linked from the Pricing section of **Commerce**), and
see the books assigned to a customer, and its contract prices, on the customer's page. Every change
asks for a person's confirmation on the [assistant](/guides/assistant/#edits-from-a-page)'s card,
which says what changes and for whom, because each changes what buyers pay:

- **New price book**: a code (lower-case letters, digits, dashes and underscores, up to 32), a
  name, a priority and optional first and last days.
- On a book's page, **Activate**, **Deactivate** and **Archive**; **Add entries**, one a line: a
  SKU, an optional quantity, then a price in USD or a percentage (`WID-100 12.50`,
  `WID-100 500 11.25`, `GSK-010 10%`); and **Assign** to a customer or a tier.

A book can be assigned only to a customer whose company is active (or that has none); taking an assignment away is always allowed, and the books already assigned to a suspended or closed company still price, since declining that company's orders is the order check's job, not pricing's.

Entries are checked before the card: a SKU must be a product, a price is 0 or more, a percentage is
above 0 and at most 100, and one call carries at most 200. Writing a SKU at a quantity already in the
book replaces it.

## Contract prices

A contract price is what was agreed with one customer for one SKU, in USD. It applies even above
list price, and a book that stops processing is reached first. Set, change or remove them (a price of
`null` removes) with `put_contract_prices` or `POST /api/v1/customers/:customerId/contract-prices`.

## Previewing a price

`preview_price` (`POST /api/v1/price-preview`) answers what a customer would pay right now for some
SKUs and quantities, and what priced each line: a book (named), a contract price, a volume tier or
the list price. Leave the customer out for an anonymous buyer. It reads only; nothing is written.

## Over REST and MCP

Everything on the page is also a REST route and an MCP tool, the same operation under both:

| Tool | Role | REST | Does |
|---|---|---|---|
| `list_price_books` | `viewer` | `GET /api/v1/price-books` | Books by code, a page at a time, optionally one status |
| `get_price_book` | `viewer` | `GET /api/v1/price-books/:bookId` | One book (by id or code), its assignees and a page of entries |
| `preview_price` | `viewer` | `POST /api/v1/price-preview` | What a customer would pay for SKUs |
| `create_price_book` | `operator` | `POST /api/v1/price-books` | Adds a draft |
| `set_price_book_status` | `operator` | `POST /api/v1/price-books/:bookId/status` | Activates, deactivates or archives |
| `put_price_book_entries` | `operator` | `POST /api/v1/price-books/:bookId/entries` | Adds, replaces or removes entries |
| `assign_price_book` | `operator` | `POST /api/v1/price-books/:bookId/assignments` | Assigns to a customer or tier, or takes it away |
| `put_contract_prices` | `operator` | `POST /api/v1/customers/:customerId/contract-prices` | Sets or removes contract prices |

The writes change what buyers pay, so only a person confirms them: an API token is refused over
REST, and a typed A2A skill never runs them. Lists page by a cursor: pass `next` back as `after`.
